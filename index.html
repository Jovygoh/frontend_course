Simulating Human Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to the development of computer systems capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence. These tasks include learning, reasoning, problem-solving, understanding natural language, and perceiving environments.

  • Machine Learning: Systems that learn from data without being explicitly programmed.
  • Neural Networks: Computing systems inspired by the biological neural networks of animal brains.
  • Generative AI: Models like ChatGPT that create new text, images, and media.
